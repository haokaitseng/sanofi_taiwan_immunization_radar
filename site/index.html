Methodology

How the radar is built, what it can and cannot tell you, and the rules it follows.

Daily pipeline

Runs by itself every day at 05:17 Taipei time on GitHub Actions. The site is a static page on GitHub Pages, so it never sleeps and needs no server.

  1. 1CollectTaiwan CDC, TFDA, Google News, PTT, Threads, comments on public Facebook pages, Wikipedia pageviews
  2. 2CleanRemove duplicates by URL and near-identical headlines, drop off-topic items such as animal influenza
  3. 3TagClaude Haiku 5.5 labels vaccine, issue, stance, speaker and storyline, and translates zh-TW to English
  4. 4AnalyseClaude Opus 5.5 writes the daily brief, explains official actions and groups storylines, citing sources for every claim
  5. 5ValidateSchema and sanity checks. If a source fails, its light turns amber and the rest of the run carries on
  6. 6PublishCommit the data to GitHub and redeploy. Git history doubles as a dated archive

Sources

Policy sources are limited to Taiwan CDC and TFDA. Social data is published only as counts, themes and paraphrases.

Definitions

Vaccine groups
Flu: influenza vaccination in general (流感疫苗). Enhanced flu is tracked as three categories: Enhanced (加強型), when a mention covers adjuvanted or high-dose vaccines without saying which; Adjuvanted (佐劑); and High-dose (高劑量). A mention that names both types counts under both. RSV: long-acting infant antibodies including nirsevimab (Beyfortus), plus maternal and older-adult RSV vaccines. Tdap: tetanus, diphtheria and acellular pertussis boosters, including in pregnancy, and pertussis itself.
Stance scale
Adapted from the WHO SAGE vaccine-hesitancy continuum. Advocating: urges others to vaccinate. Accepting: has vaccinated or intends to. Neutral: news or information with no stance. Hesitant: delays, doubts or asks for reassurance. Opposed: rejects vaccination or spreads anti-vaccine claims.
Evidence tiers
▲ Policy: official notices from Taiwan CDC or TFDA. ■ Media: news reports. ◆ Signal: social posts. Signals show volume and narratives, never facts.
Issues
Eligibility and access · Cost and self-pay · Safety and side effects · Effectiveness and evidence · Supply and availability · Disease risk and outbreaks. One mention can touch several issues.

Limitations

  • Closed spaces such as LINE groups and private Facebook groups are invisible. Facebook coverage is limited to public pages.
  • Threads and Facebook arrive through third-party scrapers that break when platforms change. Each source has its own status light.
  • Dcard is not covered: no reliable, permitted collector is available for it.
  • Keyword-based collection misses posts that never name the vaccine or disease. News headlines that name no vaccine are kept only when they came from a vaccine-specific search, and the AI tagger confirms or drops them.
  • Stance and issue tags are AI-generated. A hand-checked sample is re-scored monthly, and agreement will be reported here.
  • Volumes count mentions, not people. One active account can post many times.

Privacy, compliance and AI use

  • Social posts are stored as counts, tags and short AI paraphrases. Verbatim post text is deleted once a post is tagged, and no usernames or profile data are kept, including in the public code repository.
  • Only public pages and permitted APIs are collected, within each platform’s terms and robots rules.
  • No product claims and no medical advice. Advertising prescription medicines to the public is restricted in Taiwan.
  • AI-written text is labelled and linked to its sources. Prompts and model versions are kept in the code repository.
